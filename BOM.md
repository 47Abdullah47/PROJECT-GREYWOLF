### 🛠️ Sourcing Strategy Notes for Reviewers

1. **Direct Sourcing ($86.87 USD):** The specialized 80mm 12S EDF propulsion core is requested via upfront direct grant ordering as it requires international procurement to match specific aerodynamic design parameters.
2. **Reimbursement Sourcing (Local Parts):** To eliminate international shipping delays and accelerate the build cycle, all supporting electronics (ESC, Servos, structural carbon spars), the primary 12S battery configuration, and the 5mm Depron airframe skin panels will be procured locally from regional suppliers.
3. **Total Project Financial Footprint:** ~$146.33 USD + Local Materials (PKR 55,100 total for local components). 100% of the hardware stack is tied directly to operational flight capability.
